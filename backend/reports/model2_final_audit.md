A comprehensive variable-by-variable inspection was conducted against `src/shortage_features.py`, `src/demand_features.py`, and `data/processed/shortage_feature_matrix.parquet`.

| Feature Family | Variables Audited | Temporal Availability at Day $t$ | Future Information Leakage? |

### Strict Future Leakage Checks
1. **$t+1, t+2, t+3$ Inventory Levels**: Not present in feature columns. `current_units` reflects inventory at timestamp `00:00` of date $t$.
2. **Future Request Outcomes**: Target labels (`shortage_24h`, `shortage_48h`, `shortage_72h`) are computed from future requests but completely isolated from feature matrix `X`.
3. **Future Realized Demand**: Model 1 forecast values are generated by trained regressors; actual future `units_demanded` at $t+1, t+2, t+3$ are nowhere present in the input feature set.
4. **Future Replenishment Outcomes**: Realized transfers occurring during $[t+1, t+3]$ are not present in features.

## 3. Feature Audit

### 3.1 Nearby Inventory Feature Semantics (`available_nearby_units`)
- **Current Implementation**:
  In `data/processed/inventory_snapshots.parquet`, `available_nearby_units` is calculated by finding all regional blood banks within a 25 km Haversine distance and summing their entire inventory across all 8 blood groups and all 4 blood components.
- **Why it is Problematic**:
  A hospital evaluating a critical $O^-$ Red Blood Cell shortage will observe a high `available_nearby_units` if a neighboring blood bank has abundant stocks of $AB^+$ Plasma or $A^+$ Platelets, even if zero compatible $O^-$ RBC units are available for transfer.
- **Material Impact on Model Validity**:
  **Low to Moderate**. Feature importance analysis (Gain ranking) demonstrates that XGBoost primarily splits on:
  1. `current_units` (Gain: 833.9)
  2. `component` (Gain: 788.6)
  3. `inventory_days_remaining` (Gain: 759.3)
  4. `blood_group` (Gain: 519.4)
  5. `units_expiring_5d` (Gain: 484.2)
  `available_nearby_units` ranks as a secondary contextual signal (Gain: ~90.2), meaning it does not override local runway deficits or invert clinical shortage predictions.
- **Recommended Correction for Future Revision**:
  Refactor the snapshot extraction to compute `available_compatible_nearby_units` by applying the medical ABO/Rh compatibility matrix per blood group and component type.

### 3.2 Incoming Supply Features (`incoming_units_24h`, `48h`, `72h`)
- **Inspection Finding**:
  Features named `incoming_units_24h`, `incoming_units_48h`, and `incoming_units_72h` exist in the matrix.
- **Formula Used**:
  $$\text{incoming\_units\_24h} = \max\left(0, (\text{PAR\_LEVEL\_DAYS} \times \text{rolling\_mean\_7}) - \text{current\_units}\right)$$
  $$\text{incoming\_units\_48h} = 1.5 \times \text{incoming\_units\_24h}, \quad \text{incoming\_units\_72h} = 2.0 \times \text{incoming\_units\_24h}$$
- **Clarification**:
  These features represent **par-level buffer replenishment targets** rather than live, GPS-tracked dispatch manifests (`confirmed_incoming_24h`).
- **Reporting Stance**:
  Official documentation and system reports must **NOT** claim that confirmed live in-transit shipments were tracked. They must accurately describe these features as *modeled par-level replenishment expectations*.

## 4. Target Audit

### Target Construction
The targets are extracted from `blood_requests.csv` via `extract_future_shortage_labels()`:
- `shortage_24h`: Shortage occurring at $t+1$ (shift $-1$)
- `shortage_48h`: Shortage occurring in $[t+1, t+2]$ ($\max(\text{shift}(-1), \text{shift}(-2))$)
- `shortage_72h`: Shortage occurring in $[t+1, t+2, t+3]$ ($\max(\text{shortage\_48h}, \text{shift}(-3))$)

### Monotonicity Verification
Because any shortage within 24h is also a shortage within 48h and 72h, the condition:
$$\text{shortage\_24h} \le \text{shortage\_48h} \le \text{shortage\_72h}$$
holds across 100% of rows in the feature matrix without exception (verified in unit tests). Target columns are completely masked and never passed into `pipeline.predict()` or `pipeline.predict_proba()`.

## 6. Calibration Audit

- **Method**: Non-parametric `IsotonicRegression(out_of_bounds="clip")`.
- **Fitting Split**: Fitted strictly on validation set predictions:
  ```python
  val_raw_probs = clf.predict_proba(X_val)[:, 1]
  iso_cal.fit(val_raw_probs, y_val)
  ```
- **Test Set Contamination**: **Zero**. The test set was never seen during isotonic fitting or threshold tuning. Test set probabilities are computed via `iso_cal.predict(test_raw_probs)`.
- **Threshold Selection**: Optimal F1 decision thresholds (targeting $\text{Recall} \ge 0.50$) were derived solely on the calibrated validation probabilities ($th = 0.20$).

## 8. Known Limitations

1. **Aggregated Nearby Bank Inventory**: As identified in Check 2, `available_nearby_units` does not differentiate by component or compatible blood group.
2. **Modeled Replenishment Buffer**: Incoming supply is a heuristic par-level buffer rather than live courier telemetry.
3. **Emergency Multiplier Granularity**: Multipliers apply at the hospital facility level; individual trauma surgical spikes are smoothed across daily aggregates.
4. **False Positive Burden at 24h**: Due to severe class imbalance (4.06% positive rate), precision at 24h is 15.4%. In operational terms, roughly 6 out of 7 alerts are preventive false alarms, which is acceptable in clinical safety systems but requires tiered escalation (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`).

---

## 9. Recommended Future Improvements

1. **Compatibility-Filtered Regional Supply**: Compute `available_compatible_nearby_units` by evaluating exact donor-to-recipient blood compatibility.
2. **Dynamic In-Transit Telemetry**: Connect live transfer manifests from `data/raw/transfers.csv` with estimated arrival ETAs to create `confirmed_incoming_24h`.
3. **Hierarchical Multi-Task Learning**: Train a unified multi-task network predicting 24h, 48h, and 72h horizons simultaneously with structural monotonicity constraints.
4. **Cost-Sensitive Loss Tuning**: Incorporate asymmetric misclassification penalties directly into the XGBoost custom objective function.
